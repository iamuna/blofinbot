The signal has roughly zero edge before costs on every timeframe; costs make it lose everywhere, catastrophically on 1m–5m where stops are a few dollars wide. Its paper mode did not charge fees or slippage, which hid this. At its fixed 1.45% risk per trade with up to 10 concurrent positions, the account would not survive.

Worth keeping from it: the BloFin client (signing, retries only on GET, lot/tick quantisation), Hedge + Multi-Position handling with `positionId` reconciliation and risk reserved for unresolved orders, spread and stale-entry filters, duplicate-signal keys, PAPER/DEMO/LIVE gates.

## 2. What the 1-minute timeframe can and cannot do

Round-trip cost is ~0.16% of price. A 1m ATR on BTC is ~0.04–0.08%, so any 1m-scale stop pays 2–4R in costs per trade. The engine's risk governor therefore rejects any entry whose round-trip cost would exceed 0.35R. The bot still runs on a 1-minute clock — it checks every closed minute, manages stops and targets on 1m bars, and logs what it sees — but the signals that survive costs come from 1h and higher.

## 3. Starting champion (`lab/champion.json`)

Found by the genetic optimiser on 2022-10 → 2025-04 (training split into 4 folds that each had to hold up), ranked on 2025-04 → 2025-10 (validation), and then checked on 2025-10 → 2026-10 — a year the search never saw, during which BTC fell from $112k to $82k.

**Volatility breakout · 1h, long only.** Enter on a closed 1h candle when price closes above the 20-period 2.5σ Bollinger band or EMA 9 crosses above EMA 100 — only in a high-volatility regime, with EMA 21 > 37 > 50 and a positive MACD histogram. Swing stop, breakeven at +0.75R, target 1.5R, exit after 60 hours. Managed on every 1m bar.

It starts on **probation at half risk** because the validation window had only 15 trades and the gate asks for 20; the bot promotes it only if it passes the gate on the trading venue's own data during an hourly re-think.

Candidates that looked good on one window and failed the unseen year (kept for the record): a 1h Bollinger/harmonic long strategy found on 2022-10 → 2025-10 (−0.23R on the unseen year) and several 4h Bollinger/Fib strategies (−0.15 to −0.34R).

## 4. Caveats

- Venue: research uses Binance spot; the bot trades a perpetual feed (BloFin first, then Crypto.com, then Binance). Funding is not modelled.
- Sample sizes are modest (~40 trades a year). Expect long flat periods and losing streaks.
- Nothing here is a promise of future returns. The paper bot exists to measure this forward before any real money.
