## What it means

- **Most signals have no edge even before costs** (gross bps ≈ 0 or negative) on 5–45 minute bars. Costs (8–12 bps a round trip) then make every one of them lose. This matches our earlier test of ~60,000 one-minute BTC strategies and the published 15-minute reversal study (≈1 bp average edge).
- **The only positive line is `it-45-long`** (45-minute breakouts, long only, held about 19 hours): +0.14 R a trade, t 2.5. It is not trusted: it is the best of 29 tries (the best of 29 random strategies reaches t ≈ 2 by luck), its test-period t is 1.1, and it is long-only in a universe chosen by *today's* volume — these 31 coins rose 80% on average over the period (median +14%), so long trades ride that selection bias.
- **Longer holds do better**: every step from minutes toward a day improved results. The edge that exists is the days-to-months trend the current bot already trades.

## What the swarm does with this

- All strategies stay in **shadow** (ideas tracked on live data, no money). `it-45-long` and the two `it-45-hold*` variants are added in shadow so live evidence builds up.
- The promotion gate (≥ 200 demo trades, t ≥ 2 after costs, drawdown within 2× backtest) stays as designed: nothing trades with money until it passes.
- The strategy factory keeps searching, with every try logged and the bar raised for each extra try. New data the candle tests never had — BloFin order books, liquidations, open-interest changes — is being recorded from day 1 and is where a short-horizon edge, if any, would come from.

Reproduce: `node swarm/src/backtest/run.js --data <swarm-data checkout> --variants <variants.json> --split 2026-09-01`.
