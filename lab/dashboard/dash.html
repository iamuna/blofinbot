One-minute signals cannot pay for themselves: a 1m stop is a few dollars wide, so fees cost several times the risk. The bot therefore checks every minute — stops, targets, breakeven, data freshness — but takes entries from closed 1-hour candles.

Research and paper trading only, not financial advice. Simulated fills assume the next candle's open for entries and the stop first when stop and target share a candle; funding and exchange outages are not modeled. Bot state syncs here from the paper-state branch of iamuna/blofinbot; the live price comes from your Crypto.com connector.
